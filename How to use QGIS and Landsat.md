How to use QGIS and Landsat to track changes in urban and industrial development over time 

This methodology was developed for a study to be published as 'Destruction and protection: competing heritage regimes on Murujuga National Park (Western Australia)' by Dr José Antonio González Zarandona (Newcastle University), Dr Katrina Grant (The University of Sydney) and Dr Coral Montero Lopez (Terra Rosa Consulting).

The original methodology was developed with the support for 'Forensic Architecture' https://forensic-architecture.org/

There are many resources online to support use of QGIS and Landsat imagery. Below we outline a basic approach in brief of the steps required to reproduce our work.
	1. To set up QGIS 
	2. Find and download Landsat images from 1973 up to 2024.
	3. Import Landsat images to QGIS
	4. Techniques for identifying urban and industrial development in Landsat imagery
	5. How to draw shapefiles in QGIS around areas of interest
	6. How we demonstrated a growing Urban and Industrial footprint
